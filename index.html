06 — The Studio

Designing with intention.

Six practices guide every project we take on, from the first sketch to the final walkthrough.

01

Spatial Planning

We begin with how a space is used, not how it looks — movement, proportion and light dictate the layout before a single finish is chosen.

02

Material Selection

Stone, wood and metal are chosen for how they age, not just how they photograph — durability is part of the design brief.

03

Lighting

Layered, considered lighting shapes mood across the day — ambient, task and accent light are planned as a single system.

04

Functionality

Beautiful spaces that don't work for daily life are a failure of design — every detail earns its place.

05

Craftsmanship

We work closely with fabricators and artisans through execution, not just design — the build is where the design is proven.

06

Personalization

No two clients live the same way — each project is shaped around the people who will actually occupy it.
